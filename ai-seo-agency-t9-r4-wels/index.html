What to review before hiring

A useful first conversation should cover your current SEO tasks and how they fit into the rest of the business. You can prepare a short list of activities that repeat often, take longer than expected, or depend on information being copied between tools.

Before you speak with an agency, consider this checklist:

  • Note which SEO tasks happen daily, weekly, or monthly.
  • Gather examples of reports, content plans, or other routine work.
  • Identify where a person has to repeat the same steps.
  • Decide what a better process would let your team do with the saved time.

Avoid choosing an AI tool just because it is popular, or automating a task before you understand what it is meant to accomplish. A professional can help sort out where automation may fit and where human review still matters. Bring questions about how a proposed solution would work with the systems you already rely on.
